## Seven Years (Draft)

The owner's 7-year arc (2026-11-09). Every story year starts on the Monday of ISO week 45, the week the 4-week test starts, so the arc is measured in weeks, never dates. The career beats are my picks from each character's *Wants* in CHARACTERS.md, for the owner to change.

### The Calendar, and the Leap Year

| Story year | From | To | Length | Calendar event |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Mon 2 Nov 2026 | Sun 7 Nov 2027 | 53 weeks | ISO week 53 of 2026 (28 Dec 2026 to 3 Jan 2027) |
| 2 | Mon 8 Nov 2027 | Sun 5 Nov 2028 | 52 weeks | Leap day: **Tuesday** 29 February 2028, an ordinary contract day |
| 3 | Mon 6 Nov 2028 | Sun 4 Nov 2029 | 52 weeks | — |
| 4 | Mon 5 Nov 2029 | Sun 3 Nov 2030 | 52 weeks | — |
| 5 | Mon 4 Nov 2030 | Sun 2 Nov 2031 | 52 weeks | — |
| 6 | Mon 3 Nov 2031 | Sun 31 Oct 2032 | 52 weeks | Leap day: **Sunday** 29 February 2032, nobody's contract day |
| 7 | Mon 1 Nov 2032 | Sun 6 Nov 2033 | 53 weeks | ISO week 53 of 2032 (27 Dec 2032 to 2 Jan 2033) |

**How the leap year is handled:**

1. **Count in weeks, not dates.** Every story year starts on a Monday. A leap day is just a day inside a week, and the two 53-week years absorb the drift, so a story year never starts more than a few days from 2 November.
2. **Days and hours are leap-proof already.** R8's 7 days, R10's 30 days and R21's 168 hours count elapsed time, from server time (R29).
3. **Months clamp to the month's last day.** The 6-month overtime expiry and the 24-month windows step by calendar month; a day that doesn't exist becomes the last day of that month. 29 February 2028 plus 24 months is 28 February 2030; 31 August 2027 plus 6 months is 29 February 2028.
4. **Contracts are by weekday** (O4), so a leap day is simply whatever weekday it falls on: worked in 2028, a day off for everyone in 2032.
5. **Yearly resets follow the calendar year** in the person's own time zone (ghost links, every January), so a leap year just has one more day to click in.

A full calendar cycle is 28 years (7 weekdays × the 4-year leap cycle), and 400 years for good, because 2100 skips its leap day. Seven years never repeats: 1 January falls on Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday. 2028's leap day skips Sunday.

### Found by the Seven Years

1. **Nobody can leave voluntarily.** A membership's `endReason` is `Replaced`, `Declined`, `Removed`, `AccountDeleted`, `CompanyDeleted` or `Forfeited`. Malik resigning, Victor and Dale retiring, Daniel and Sipho dropping a company: none of them fits. **Settled (owner, 2026-11-09):** `Left` was added, ended by the person themselves, and it counts as the owner's turnover (R28).
2. **Month arithmetic needs the clamping rule above** written into the rules, or 29 February 2028 has no 24-month anniversary.
